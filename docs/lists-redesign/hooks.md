### Collisions between the spec's new texts and the checks (the built text wins)

1. **Empty page.** C147 needs `#view` textContent to **start** with the built sentence. The spec's kicker "No lists yet" and headline "Nothing to make yet" come first in reading order. The built sentence must stay first in the page text; kicker and headline can be drawn with generated content or `order`, but not placed before it in the document.
2. **"For which character?".** C158 and C164 read the button text "Alba: 3 not ticked". The spec draws the name left and the count (display numeral) right with a chevron. The text content of the button must still read "<name>: <n> not ticked" (a visually drawn numeral with the words "not ticked" in the button's accessible text is not enough: the check reads textContent).
3. **Hide completed.** C153 and C155 read `[data-act=hide]` and expect the text "Show completed" while hidden. A labelled switch whose label stays "Hide completed" fails them. Keep a `[data-act=hide]` button whose text toggles (the switch row may be that button).
4. **Craft row facts.** C153 reads "3 crafts, makes 9" from the row; the spec's line 2 reads "makes 9" and the count block holds "3 / crafts". The row's text must still contain "3 crafts, makes 9". C168 to C172 read "need 3," and "have 4" (a comma after the figure); the spec's "need 9 · have 3" uses a dot. Keep the comma form in the row text.
5. **Undo.** The built button reads "Undo: <change>". The spec's status line has the change as a sentence and a separate link "Undo". Checks click `[data-act=undo][data-i="0"]` (selector, not text), so the link keeps those two attributes; the earlier-changes list keeps `data-i` 1, 2, and so on.
6. **Combined tick and "Open".** C152 reads the checkbox `[data-f=tick]` in `.list-card` and clicks `[data-act=open]`. The spec makes the whole card the link: keep `[data-act=open]` on that link.
7. **Card text.** C149 and others find a card by its text (`.list-card` with `hasText` the list name); "More for <list name>" and "In combined view" do not collide.
8. Not colliding: "List settings", "Done", "Show all N changes", "made <day month>", "Game data changed" (the stamp; the notice title keeps its built sentence in `h2`), "Must craft" (the tick keeps "Must be crafted by me").

## 3. How things behave today

- **New list.** `[data-act=new]` (button "New list", on Your lists and in its empty state) shows an inline form `form[data-form=new]` in the page: a text box `[data-f=newname]` (label "List name", `maxlength` 60, no autocomplete), a select `[data-f=newchar]` (every character by name, then "No character", which is the last option), the button "Create" (`data-act=create`) and "Cancel" (`data-act=cancel-new`). Create makes the list, opens it (the list bar with `[data-f=listname]` appears) and saves at once. Nothing is created before Create.
- **Settings controls save when they change, with no save button.** `[data-f=listname]` (text) on `change` (blur or Enter); `[data-f=listchar]` and `[data-f=priceWorld]` (selects) on `change`; "Add a step" (`[data-act=addstep]`, with the text box `[data-f=stepname]`) on click; "Hide completed" (`[data-act=hide]`, `aria-pressed`) on click (kept in the save's prefs); "Order of ways" (`[data-act=sheet]`, `aria-expanded`) opens the sheet on click and each "Move up" and "Move down" (`data-act=order-up|order-down`, `data-way`) saves on click; "Back to the usual order" is `[data-act=order-reset]`. Every saved change is queued and written with the page's one save (`state.busy`, `state.queue`), becomes an undo entry (at most 20), and redraws the page.
- **Status message.** One `div.lists-status[role=status][aria-live=polite]` at the top of the page. `state.note` (a sentence, or a refusal beginning "Not saved on this device: " or "Undo was not done: ") is cleared by the next successful save and by opening a list, the combined view or the crafting log; a refusal stays until then. Nothing is timed. Undo entries are kept (up to 20, newest first) until used or the page is closed: they are not cleared by time. The only timer is `setInterval(tickWindows, 15000)`, which rewrites the minutes of timed-node windows without a redraw.
- **"Earlier changes".** Drawn when more than one undo entry exists: the newest entry is the button "Undo: <label>" (`data-act=undo`, `data-i=0`); the rest are `<details class="lists-earlier"><summary>Earlier changes (<n>)</summary><ul><li><span><label></span> <button data-act=undo data-i=<i>>Undo</button></li>…</ul></details>`, closed at first. "Buy the rest too: from a merchant" or "…: on the market" is a `[data-act=rest]` button (`data-by`) in `p.lists-rest`.
- **Redraw.** `render()` rewrites `#view` after every save, so an open drawer or sheet has to be kept in `state.view` to survive (B3 escalates if it cannot).
