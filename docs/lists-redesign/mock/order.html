Order of ways

When an item can be got in several ways, Tamsin Reed’s lists try them in this order:

  1. 1Buy from a merchant (gil)Buy from a merchant for gil. If an ordinary merchant sells it for gil, you buy it, and the materials to craft it are left out.
  2. 2Craft itCraft it.
  3. 3Gather itGather it.
  4. 4Trade for other currenciesTrade for it with another currency (scrips, tomestones, seals, tokens). These are not counted as easy, so they come after crafting and gathering unless you move them up.
  5. 5Buy on the market boardBuy on the market board.

This order is used in all of Tamsin Reed’s lists. You can change it here, or choose a way for one item on its row, at any time. Items from the crafting log, and collectables, are always crafted.
