# Submersibles: colour roles

Generated by tracker/tools/subs-roles.js. Do not edit.

Source: docs/submersibles-look-spec.md, Revision 4 (Rev4.4 to Rev4.6). The palette model is `tracker/command-center/src/submersibles/palette.js`; the tool reads it, the theme rows of `look.js` and the token rules of `subs.css`. Hex values are the colours the model computes at the default contrast, for each of the 21 themes in light and dark. A tone the model cannot compute is printed as its formula or as "set by the theme".

## 1. The roles

Tones: `field` a solid ground that carries text; `on` the text on that field; `graphic` bands, bars, borders and marks (3:1 on the page colours); `text` coloured text (4.5:1 on the page colours); `wash` a tint under ink text (7:1); `band` a gradient band; `glow` a glow; `shade` a shade over anything.

**27 roles, 102 tones** (9 theme roles with 25 tones; 18 meaning roles with 77 tones, counting the shared `--on-mid`). A theme role comes from the theme. A meaning role has one hue family in every theme (rank 232 degrees, gil 92, loot 188, route 340; the statuses, error, the seven seas and the chart keep the hues they always had) and reaches the theme through lightness and chroma only.

The floors the test asserts in all 42 pairs of theme and mode: a role to any status 20 degrees; a role to a role 40; a role to a sea 15; a status to a status 35; a sea to a sea 30. A sea to a status is printed, not asserted. Where hue cannot keep two things apart, form does: a sea is a round lettered disc, a tab, a time cell or an edge beside its name; a status is a slanted field with its word.
