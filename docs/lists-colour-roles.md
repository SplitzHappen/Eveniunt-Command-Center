## 1. The roles

Tones: `field` a solid ground that carries text; `on` the text on that field; `graphic` bands, bars, borders and marks (3:1 on the page colours); `text` coloured text (4.5:1 on the page colours); `wash` a tint under ink text (7:1); `band` a gradient band; `shade` a shade over anything.

**15 roles, 43 tones** (8 theme roles, the six meaning roles and the shared text tone `--on-mid`). A theme role comes from the theme (`look.js`, `shared.css`). A meaning role has one hue in every theme (craft 250, gather 140, merchant 80, market 315, ready 195, short 25 degrees, OKLCH); the theme reaches it through the ink and panel colours it is mixed with, so only lightness and strength move. A meaning is always also a word or a shape (design rule K4).
