## Appearance findings (first, as asked)

1. **NOTE — the job icons are flat silhouettes, not the game's coloured icons.** The owner asked for "the crafters and gatherers icons from the game … just like teamcraft does them"; Teamcraft shows the coloured pictures. What shipped uses the game's icon *shapes* as a CSS mask painted in the row's ink colour (`tracker/command-center/src/lists/lists.css:114`, the `.jicon[src$=…]` mask rules below it, `jobImg` in `lists.js`), so every icon is one flat colour that fits every theme and passes the 3:1 graphic contrast test. On the screenshots the shapes are clearly the game's (hammer, flask, pick…) with the three-letter abbreviation under each. Consequence for a player: none wrong, but it reads flatter than Teamcraft's coloured icons and is a visible departure from what was asked. Fix if wanted: drop the mask and show the PNG as-is (the pictures are already dark-on-transparent and small); that trades away the guaranteed contrast in all 21 themes. Evidence: `lists.css:114`, `lists-roles.test.js` ("job pictures are drawn in the row's ink through a mask"), screenshots `craft-desktop-light.png`, `get-gather-desktop-light.png`.
2. **NOTE — the zone and city sub-heads inside Gather and Buy-from-a-merchant are 14 px tall strips** (`lists.css:660`, `.zone-head { height: 14px }`). They carry the zone name and row count and were legible in `get-gather-desktop-light.png` and `project-desktop-light.png`, but they are the tightest element on the screens; the 44 px head minimum in the house style covers section heads, not these. Consequence: none functional; a little cramped by eye. Fix if wanted: give them ~24–28 px. Evidence: `lists.css:660`, `get-gather-desktop-light.png`.
3. **NOTE — on a phone, a gather row at rest hides the node name, the aetheryte and "need N, have N"** (by design, red-team finding 15: `lists.css:541` hides `.sp-n`, `.sp-a`, `.w-need` under 700 px; the count block carries the number and the opened row has the whole spot line). I verified the opened gather drawer shows every spot with node name, coordinates, aetheryte, window and folklore (`get-gather-open-desktop-light.png`, `get-gather-open-phone-dark.png`). No action needed; recorded so the owner knows it was deliberate. Evidence: `lists.css:541`, `get-gather-phone-dark.png`.
4. **NOTE — To craft can show the same job as two numbered heads** when the plan's craft order interleaves jobs (the engine builds blocks of one job, greedily: `engine.js:419-435`; the page only merges consecutive same-job blocks). In `craft-hq-desktop-light.png` Leatherworker appears as heads 5 and 7, numbered in craft order. Reads as "craft in this order", not as a duplicate. No action needed. Evidence: `craft-hq-desktop-light.png`, `engine.js:419`.
5. **MINOR — the trade sentence is terse: "Traded by Ixali vendor in North Shroud: 1 Ixali Oaknot for 2."** The "for 2" means "for 2 of the item on this row"; the row name sits right above, so it is readable, but the opened drawer's "Chosen trade" line repeats it bare. Fix: end the sentence with the item name ("…for 2 Coke."). Evidence: `lists.js:670` (`offerText`), `project-desktop-light.png` (Coke row).
6. **NOTE — phone budgets are met, one borderline:** `measures.json` shows phone rows ≤ 96 px at rest and ≤ 360 px opened, ≤ 2 controls and 1 primary per row, no sideways scroll, first row 175–210 px below the masthead. The Wanted screen's first row is at 210 px — over the 200 px budget but inside the 256 px allowance for a screen with a strip (its add field is the screen's purpose field). Desktop rows ≤ 64 px, 9–11 rows in view. Evidence: `docs/lists-redesign/build/measures.json`.

Overall look judgement: every screen I inspected follows `docs/design-rules.md` and matches the approved mock-ups — masthead with labelled "Main menu", rail from 701 px / bottom bar on phone (and no bar at all when there is one entry), the inverted slab list head with the two (or three) tallies as the largest element, count blocks with the fixed 26 px slash that never touches the name, one ledger per section under a solid head that says its count, quiet outline primaries on rows (one filled primary per sheet or notice), the fixed status line as a slim line with underlined text links (not a button), sheets with labelled close / tap-outside / Escape, the full empty state with a decoration that invents nothing, roles by hue always with a word, no monospace labels, no sideways scroll at 360 px. None of the rejected "before" faults (rows of bordered buttons, the always-open form, monospace labels, button-shaped status, a box per row, sparse cards) reappears in `build/`.

## Code, facts, behaviour, safety findings

7. **MAJOR (already a documented known limit) — the "closes …, in N min" part of a timed node's window is not proved against the game.** The data tool keeps Teamcraft's window length (`tracker/tools/lists-data.py:515,526`, `(n.get("duration") or 120)`), and `SOURCES.md` says plainly the sheet's own `Duration` is "in another unit and was NOT proved" (values like 300 in `GatheringRarePopTimeTable` against Teamcraft's 120/180). I re-fetched the sheet at the pinned commit: start times look like Eorzea minutes (e.g. 900 = 15:00) and durations like 300; the start hours of all 195 timed nodes were already proved to match. Consequence for a player: "opens 14:00 Eorzea time" is solid, but "open now, closes 16:00, in 6 min" can be wrong about when the node closes, so a player can be sent to a node that has already closed (or told to wait for one that is open). Fix: prove the `Duration` unit once (one in-game observation or the sheet's unit), use the sheet value with a stop rule, or drop the closing time and show only "opens X Eorzea time". Evidence: `lists-data.py:515,526`, `docs/SOURCES.md` "Window" note, `docs/STATE.md` Known limits, my fetch of `GatheringRarePopTimeTable.csv` at the pinned commit, screenshots (`get-gather-open-desktop-light.png` shows "opens 00:00 Eorzea time, in 1 min").
8. **MINOR — the item-name lookup for sync questions is built and tested but never wired up.** `save.js:303` takes an optional `itemName` lookup and falls back to "item 5056" (tested in `tracker/tests/lists-patch.test.js`, R6-2), but every production caller — `merge.js:392,394` in all three apps, and the Command Center's Settings page — calls `describeUnit` without it (I grepped every call site; only tests pass six arguments). So sync questions name Lists items by number everywhere, not only in the Tracker as `docs/STATE.md`'s known limit says. Consequence: in a rare cross-device clash the question says "the amount Tamsin Reed has of item 5056" instead of the item's name; the player must look the number up. Fix: pass a name lookup from the Command Center (it can read the Lists data) at the two `merge.js` call sites. Evidence: `save.js:303-330`, `merge.js:392-394`, `lists-patch.test.js:77-89`, `docs/STATE.md` Known limits.
9. **NOTE — no check or test was weakened without a stated reason.** The removed lines in `check.js` are reach edits (open the row / sheet before clicking), which the look spec explicitly allows; the one changed assertion (C166) turns "about 0 gil for 0 items" into "No market prices yet: 1 item without a price", a deliberate fix of house rule S5 (unknown is never shown as 0). `tests/lists-look.test.js` was replaced by `lists-roles.test.js`, which keeps every old assertion (4.5:1 contrast in all 21 themes both modes, sRGB, hue floors, no raw colour outside the token block, a word per role, 40 px phone tap targets, status line not a button, data face on figures only) and adds generated-document equality, pointer checks and the icon-mask contrast check; only the phone breakpoint assertion moved from 480 px to the design's 700 px. `DR1` in `check.js` breaks each design rule on purpose once and asserts the helper catches it (`DR1_BREAKS`, `check.js:8367-8409`). Evidence: `git diff cfae181 HEAD -- tracker/command-center/check.js tracker/tests/`.
10. **NOTE — Wanted phone first row at 210 px** (see finding 6); within the strip exception. No action.

## Choices for the owner (every option, including keeping things as they are)

**A. The closing time of a timed gathering node (finding 7).**
- Keep things as they are: the row keeps "open now, closes 16:00 Eorzea time, in 6 min" from Teamcraft's length. What it improves: nothing new. What it costs: nothing. Risk left: the "closes/in N min" text can be wrong (start times are verified, length is not) — a player can be sent to a closed node; odds unknown because the unit was never proved. What you give up: the precise closing time. How sure I am: sure the start time is right, unsure the length is.
- Prove the sheet's duration unit once and use the game's value (recommended): one in-game observation of a node's open and close, or reading the unit of `GatheringRarePopTimeTable.Duration`, then the tool stops if it ever disagrees. What it improves: the closing time becomes game data, not an assumption. Cost: a little time once, after the next patch too. Risk left: low. What you give up: nothing. How sure: fairly sure it is a one-off check.
- Show only "opens 14:00 Eorzea time" and drop the closing time: zero risk of a wrong closing time, cost is a less informative row (players like knowing how long a node stays). Risk left: none. How sure: sure.
- Recommendation: prove the unit and use the game's value; if that cannot be done before publishing, fall back to showing only the opening time.
- Technical: `tracker/tools/lists-data.py:515,526`; `GatheringRarePopTimeTable` at `xivapi/ffxiv-datamining@44948e4c…/csv/en/GatheringRarePopTimeTable.csv`; `docs/SOURCES.md` "Window"; `docs/STATE.md` Known limits.

**B. Item names in sync questions (finding 8).**
- Keep things as they are: clash questions say "item 5056". Cost: the player looks the number up in the list. Risk: a wrong choice in a rare question is harder to check; nothing is deleted either way (the question still asks before anything changes). How sure: sure it only affects wording.
- Wire the lookup in the Command Center (recommended): pass a name map to `describeUnit` at the two `merge.js` call sites; the Tracker keeps numbers until it loads the Lists data. Cost: a small change plus rebuild of the three apps (as `CLAUDE.md` requires for save-code changes). Risk left: a name the lookup misses still shows a number. What you give up: nothing. How sure: fairly sure.
- Recommendation: wire it in the Command Center; it already has the data.
- Technical: `tracker/src/core/save.js:303`; `tracker/src/core/merge.js:392,394`; `tracker/tests/lists-patch.test.js:77-89`.

**C. The flat job icons (finding 1).**
- Keep things as they are: game icon shapes in the theme's ink, contrast guaranteed in all 21 themes. Cost: looks flatter than Teamcraft's coloured icons you asked for. Risk: none. How sure: sure.
- Show the game's coloured icons as-is (recommended only if you want the Teamcraft look): drop the mask on `.jicon`. Improves: exactly the look you described. Cost: contrast in all themes must be re-measured per icon (the test exists and will say); some icons may fail on some themes and need a per-theme exception. Risk: a few icons could read poorly on a light theme. What you give up: the guaranteed 3:1 in every theme without extra work. How sure: moderately sure.
- Recommendation: keep the masked icons for this publish (they are unmistakably the game's shapes and always readable); revisit if you miss the colour.
- Technical: `tracker/command-center/src/lists/lists.css:114` and the `.jicon[src$=…]` rules; `tracker/tests/lists-roles.test.js` mask test; `docs/lists-look-spec.md` D-C2.
