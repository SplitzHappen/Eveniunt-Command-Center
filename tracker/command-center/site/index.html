Welcome back

Pick a module. The Tracker and the Atelier open exactly as you know them; the Command Center link in each module's header brings you back here.

Module Tracker Completion lists for up to eight characters side by side, plus the Live boards: timers, dailies, hunts, weather, market board and more. Open the Tracker → Module Atelier Crafting solver: recipe search, crafter stats, solver, simulator, macros and a step-by-step guide. Opens as its own page so it can use every processor core. Open the Atelier → Module Submersibles Your free companies' submarines on one board: Back, Out, Idle or Locked with live countdowns, the return times you type from the game, alerts and calendar entries.
